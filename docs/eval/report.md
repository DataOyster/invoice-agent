## Notes

- Matching rule: a flag counts as correct when the same invoice has an injected anomaly of the same type; several flags of one type on one invoice count once.
- Standard split: the rules are near-perfect by construction (anomalies injected by rules, clean machine-generated PDFs). It shows that dataset and checks are consistent, not that the problem is solved.
- Hard split: an unseen layout, product names printed differently from the catalog, strong rotation and scans without a text layer. The rules were not changed to fit it.
- At most one injected anomaly per invoice; real invoices can carry several at once.
- Contracts are in English only; invoices are in Swedish, English and Italian.
