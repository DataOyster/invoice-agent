## Notes

- Matching rule: a flag counts as correct when the same invoice has an injected anomaly of the same type; several flags of one type on one invoice count once.
- The baseline is near-perfect by construction: anomalies are injected by rules, and the PDFs are clean, machine-generated text. A perfect baseline score therefore says the dataset and checks are consistent, not that the problem is solved.
- Consequence for task 7: the agent cannot beat this baseline on recall on this dataset. Its value has to be shown on what fixed rules cannot do (cited explanations, clause retrieval, unreadable or unfamiliar documents, prompt-injection resistance), and on harder variants of the data.
- At most one injected anomaly per invoice; real invoices can carry several at once.
- Contracts are in English only; invoices are in Swedish, English and Italian.
